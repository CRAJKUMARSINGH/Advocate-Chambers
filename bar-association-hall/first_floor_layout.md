# First Floor - Bar Library + Pantry

**Revision:** B - dimensioned architectural preliminary review plan

See `first_floor_preliminary.svg` for the dimensioned architectural review plan and `preliminary_plan_review.md` for the full design basis and review checklist.

## Room schedule

- **Librarian / Admin:** 9'-0" x 16'-6" at the south-west landing; issuing, supervision, and administration.
- **Dog-leg Stair Core:** 12'-0" x 16'-6" stacked over the ground-floor stair core, with a **4'-0" clear flight width**.
- **Stair geometry:** 18 risers total at 6-2/3", 11" treads, and 4'-0" landings for the assumed 10'-0" floor-to-floor height.
- **Toilet Block:** 9'-0" x 16'-6" stacked over the ground-floor toilet zone; male, female, and accessible provisions shown as an architectural planning block.
- **Library Lobby / Circulation:** 30'-0" x 8'-0" between the stair/service core and the reading room.
- **Pantry:** 10'-0" x 8'-0" stacked over the ground-floor pantry.
- **Library Reading Room:** 55'-0" x 35'-0" for tables, quiet study, and flexible seating.
- **Stack Area / Book Storage:** 55'-0" x 18'-0" for law reports, journals, and archives; confirm floor loading with the structural engineer.
- **Discussion Room:** 18'-0" x 10'-6".
- **Computer / Internet Room:** 18'-0" x 10'-6".
- **Store / Electrical:** 19'-0" x 10'-6" with final equipment clearances to be coordinated.

## Preliminary planning notes

- Stair, pantry, and wet areas are stacked for a cleaner plumbing strategy.
- First-floor visitors use the independent exterior stair entry; the route does not pass through the ground-floor hall.
- The stair annotation separates the 4'-0" clear flight width from the 11" tread depth; these are not interchangeable dimensions.
- Reading and stack zones occupy the upper wing and receive planned daylight from the north/east sides.
- The west boundary wall is shown blank with no openings.
- Confirm shelving loads, escape capacity, accessible clearances, ventilation, electrical/data loads, and fire separation before design development.
