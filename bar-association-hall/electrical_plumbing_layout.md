# Electrical and Plumbing (MEP) Layout - Both Floors

> Concept-level services brief. Final load schedules, cable sizes, pipe sizes, fire systems, and approvals belong to the project MEP/fire consultants.

## Electrical

### Power supply
- Main distribution board: 3-phase connection in the ground-floor electrical room.
- Sub-distribution board: one on the ground floor and one on the first floor.
- UPS/inverter provision in the server/electrical room.

### Lighting and fans
- Main hall/library: 2 x 2 LED panels (40 W) at an approximately 8'-0" planning grid.
- Main hall/library: 1200 mm ceiling fans at an approximately 12'-0" planning grid.
- Lobby and circulation: 20 W LED fittings plus emergency exit lighting.
- Toilets and pantry: 15 W LED fittings and 250 mm exhaust fans.
- External: low-level/bollard lighting only where the approved setback strategy permits.

### Power and data
- General 5 A and 15 A sockets at coordinated furniture heights.
- Dedicated 20 A circuits for planned split ACs in hall, library, and admin rooms; verify actual loads.
- Dedicated 16 A UPS-backed circuit for server equipment.
- Cat6 route from server room to admin, library, reception, and computer room.

## Plumbing

### Water supply
- Municipal connection plus a conceptual 2,000 L underground sump.
- Conceptual 1,000 L overhead tank above the toilet/service zone; verify structure and demand.
- CPVC supply lines concealed only after coordination with structure and waterproofing.

### Drainage and rainwater
- Stack toilet shafts between floors where practical.
- Planning pipe sizes: 4 in soil and 2 in waste lines; verify hydraulic design.
- Pantry sink waste to the main drainage line with cleanouts and traps.
- 4 in roof rainwater downpipes to a recharge/harvesting pit in an approved north or east setback area.

### Fire protection
- Ground floor: two ABC extinguishers in the hall and one in the lobby.
- First floor: two ABC extinguishers in the library and one near the stair.
- Provide approved fire-exit and extinguisher signage after the final fire strategy is issued.
