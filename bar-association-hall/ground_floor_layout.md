# Ground Floor - Bar Association Banswara Hall

**Revision:** B - dimensioned architectural preliminary review plan

See `ground_floor_preliminary.svg` for the dimensioned architectural review plan and `preliminary_plan_review.md` for the full design basis and review checklist.

## Room schedule

- **Reception / Records:** 9'-0" x 16'-6" at the south-west entry; welcome desk, waiting, and filing counter.
- **Dog-leg Stair Core:** 12'-0" x 16'-6" in the south service core. The stair is shown at **4'-0" clear flight width**, not as a 4'-0" tread.
- **Stair geometry:** 18 risers total at 6-2/3" each for a 10'-0" floor-to-floor height, 11" treads, two 4'-0" landings, and a 6" central stair wall/rail zone to be finalized by the structural designer.
- **Toilet Block:** 9'-0" x 16'-6" with male, female, and accessible provisions shown as an architectural planning block.
- **Entry Lobby / Public Circulation:** 30'-0" x 8'-0" connecting the south entry, toilet block, pantry, and the centered hall entry.
- **Pantry:** 10'-0" x 8'-0" on the east side of the lobby, with sink, counter, storage, and service door.
- **Main Assembly Hall:** 55'-0" x 49'-0" with central aisle, member seating, and clear route to the dais.
- **Dais / Speaker Zone:** 55'-0" x 14'-6" at the north end of the hall.

## Preliminary planning notes

- Public entry is from the south through the reception/records vestibule.
- The main hall door is centered on the 55'-0" south long wall of the hall and is reached from the public lobby.
- The stair has a separate 4'-0" clear external entry door on the south wall; visitors to the first floor do not pass through the ground-floor hall.
- The west boundary wall is shown blank with no openings.
- The east-side exit is a review placeholder subject to fire strategy, site access, and local approval.
- The drawing uses the 4,427.5 sq ft coordinate envelope, not the unresolved 4,785 sq ft planning claim.
- Stair width is dimensioned as a 4'-0" clear flight width; tread depth is dimensioned separately as 11".
- Validate occupant load, aisle width, accessible route, exits, daylight, ventilation, and fire provisions before design development.
