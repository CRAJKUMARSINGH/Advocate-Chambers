# Structural Grid and Framing - G+1 Building

> Concept-level planning information only. A structural engineer must size foundations, columns, beams, slabs, retaining/boundary elements, and seismic/wind detailing from a site investigation and final architectural plan.

## Foundation concept

- RCC isolated footings with plinth beam are the planning assumption.
- The brief assumes a minimum founding depth of 1.5 m below natural ground level; confirm from geotechnical information.
- The west zero-setback edge requires an independent structural and waterproofing strategy; do not treat it as a retaining wall without engineering design.

## Framing assumptions to verify

- Typical slab planning value: 125 mm (5 in).
- Beam planning sizes: 230 x 450 mm main; 230 x 300 mm secondary.
