# Common Amenities - Both Floors

## Sanitary and accessibility

- Male toilet block
- Female toilet block
- Accessible toilet
- Drinking water station
- Wash basin and hand-wash area
- Accessible route to principal rooms, toilets, and exits

## Utility

- Pantry with sink, counter, and storage
- Store room
- Electrical/server room
- First-aid room
- Structured data cabling to admin, library, reception, and server spaces

## Safety

- Fire extinguishers on each floor
- Emergency exit and extinguisher signage
- Emergency lighting at exits and circulation routes
- Fire strategy and occupant load to be approved by the responsible professional

## Zero-setback west wall

- Blank wall with no unapproved openings
- Independent structure and footing strategy
- Waterproofing and damp-proofing detailing
- Local fire-separation and boundary compliance review
